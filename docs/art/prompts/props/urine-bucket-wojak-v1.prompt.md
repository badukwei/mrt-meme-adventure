# urine-bucket-wojak-v1

Tool: built-in imagegen. Game layer: transparent prop.

Single game sprite: a wide open turquoise cleaning bucket viewed slightly from above with readable dark oval opening, thick black rim, small side handles. On its front is a large classic Wojak face drawn in black: drooping eyelids, eye bags, long simple nose, thin resigned mouth, worried brow creases. Crude uneven black MS Paint meme outlines, flat color, minimal shading. The face must immediately look like classic Wojak, not emoji or cute kawaii. Entire bucket centered, no cropping. Transparent background, no floor shadow, no words, no other objects. Production 2D game prop.
